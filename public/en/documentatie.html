Technical explanation

Different applications.
Different performance requirements.

EI

Firestopping

Firestopping ratings include EI30, EI60, EI90 and EI120. The appropriate rating and system follow from the project requirements.

R

Steel protection

Coatings for load-bearing steel can be specified for R30, R60, R90 or R120, depending on the system, structure and coating thickness.
